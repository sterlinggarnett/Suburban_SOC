## Investigation (Phase 1)

host (both removed afterwards) — the man page is not installed here, so the
`-`-prefix semantics were measured rather than recalled:

| Probe | Directive | Result | ExecStart reached |

| `soc550-nodashtest` | `ExecStartPre=/nonexistent/dangling-docker cp a b` | `status=203/EXEC` | no |

`slo-metrics.service:100` was the only ES-CA-extracting `ExecStartPre` in the
repo without the prefix (intel-refresh:121, checkpoints-compact:94,
threat-intel-compact:81 all had it).

Two further live breaks found in the same lane, both independent of #550 —
see `findings/20260906-550-slo-metrics-docker-dependency.md` §3:

    `SLO_METRICS_PASSWORD` in `.env` no longer matches ES) — every ES metric
    unmeasurable, nothing indexed to `soc-slo-metrics`, exit 3;
  - `NTFY_TOPIC` is absent from this host's `.env`, so the alert block in
    `slo_metrics.py:1969` never fires regardless of breach state.

## Noted, not actioned

`.gitattributes` forces LF for `*.sh`, `*.conf`, `.env*`, `*.cron`, `*.dat` but
NOT `*.service`/`*.timer`. With `core.autocrlf=true` on this host,
`configs/systemd/intel-refresh.service` is currently checked out with CRLF
terminators. systemd's parser strips trailing `\r`, so nothing is broken today,
and HEAD stores LF — but it is the exact hazard `.gitattributes` exists to
prevent, two globs short. Follow-up, not bundled into #550.
